Frequently Asked Questions

What is a Vickrey Second-Price Auction and why do we use it?

A Vickrey auction is a sealed or live auction mechanism where the highest bidder wins, but only pays the second-highest bid price. Nobel prize-winning economist William Vickrey demonstrated that this completely eliminates "bid sniping" and incentivizes all participants to bid their true valuation without gamesmanship.

Can I purchase a vehicle directly without waiting for the timer to expire?

Yes! Every lot features a Direct Buy reserve price. Selecting Direct Purchase executes instant settlement in the backend, closes the auction immediately, and reserves the vehicle for your legal account.

How does the bidding deposit work?

Your deposit is maintained in a refundable escrow account. When you purchase a vehicle, 100% of the deposit is credited toward your purchase balance. If you are outbid or decide not to buy, you can withdraw your full balance back to your bank account with zero deductions.

Who handles the RTO transfer paperwork?

Boli directly handles statutory Form 29 and Form 30 paperwork under Section 50 of the Motor Vehicles Act. We verify road tax clearance and deliver the transferred registration certificate directly to your doorstep.
